Excellent movie. Very weird, in a good way in various places. Some parts were expected but still overall a lot of fun. I enjoyed how the men in the movie were mostly helpless.

Because the film has a slow ramp, I got this weird feeling that the characters were headed toward doom and they didn't even know it. Sort of like when you drive the opposite way of traffic on the freeway and as you get near the end of the jam you can't help but think, "these poor bastards don't even know how far back they are." But I guess we're all on a path like this?
